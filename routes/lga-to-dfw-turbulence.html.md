# LGA to DFW turbulence

> New York (LGA) ⇄ Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between New York (LGA) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) fly about 1,390 miles, roughly 3 hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Kentucky, and more often in December, March and May.

- Distance: 1,390 miles (2,230 km)
- In the air: about 3 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 27,593
- Severe reports: 100 (0.4%)
- Gusty days: LGA 146, DFW 122

## Questions

### Is LGA to DFW a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the LGA–DFW route filed 27,593 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 100 of 27,593 (0.4%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between LGA and DFW?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Kentucky, near Nashville, about 1 h 40 min after leaving LGA, or 1 h 15 min after leaving DFW. The next most were over West Virginia, near Columbus, about 1 h after leaving LGA, or 1 h 55 min after leaving DFW.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly LGA to DFW?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in December, March and May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is DFW to LGA the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from DFW to LGA usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at LGA and DFW?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. LGA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 146 days in the past year, most often in December (20 days). DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
