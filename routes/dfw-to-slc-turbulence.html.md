# DFW to SLC turbulence

> Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) ⇄ Salt Lake City (SLC), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Salt Lake City (SLC) fly about 990 miles, roughly 2¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, and more often in December.

- Distance: 990 miles (1,590 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 10 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 15,995
- Severe reports: 45 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: DFW 122, SLC 105

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Colorado, near Colorado Springs, about 1 h 15 min after leaving DFW, or 55 min after leaving SLC. There, 545 of 1,588 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were over Texas, about 50 min after leaving DFW, or 1 h 20 min after leaving SLC. There, 419 of 881 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is DFW to SLC a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the DFW–SLC route filed 15,995 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 45 of 15,995 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between DFW and SLC?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, near Colorado Springs, about 1 h 15 min after leaving DFW, or 55 min after leaving SLC. The next most were over Texas, about 50 min after leaving DFW, or 1 h 20 min after leaving SLC.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly DFW to SLC?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in April, July and August and a bigger share in December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SLC to DFW the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from SLC to DFW usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at DFW and SLC?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days). SLC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 105 days in the past year, most often in July (14 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
