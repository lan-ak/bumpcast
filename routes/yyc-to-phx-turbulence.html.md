# YYC to PHX turbulence

> Calgary (YYC) ⇄ Phoenix (PHX), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Calgary (YYC) and Phoenix (PHX) fly about 1,230 miles, roughly 2¾ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Utah, and more often in December.

- Distance: 1,230 miles (1,970 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 40 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 4,140
- Severe reports: 22 (0.5%)
- Gusty days: YYC 121, PHX 68

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Utah, about 1 h 45 min after leaving YYC, or 50 min after leaving PHX. There, 178 of 248 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were over Idaho, near Salt Lake City, about 1 h 20 min after leaving YYC, or 1 h 15 min after leaving PHX. There, 170 of 274 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is YYC to PHX a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYC–PHX route filed 4,140 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 22 of 4,140 (0.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between YYC and PHX?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Utah, about 1 h 45 min after leaving YYC, or 50 min after leaving PHX. The next most were over Idaho, near Salt Lake City, about 1 h 20 min after leaving YYC, or 1 h 15 min after leaving PHX.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYC to PHX?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in April and July and a bigger share in December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is PHX to YYC the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYC and PHX?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 121 days in the past year, most often in June (17 days). PHX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 68 days in the past year, most often in June (13 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
