# SEA to DFW turbulence

> Seattle (SEA) ⇄ Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Seattle (SEA) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) fly about 1,660 miles, roughly 3½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, and more often in December.

- Distance: 1,660 miles (2,670 km)
- In the air: about 3 h 30 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 18,993
- Severe reports: 81 (0.4%)
- Gusty days: SEA 48, DFW 122

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Colorado, near Colorado Springs, about 2 h 15 min after leaving SEA, or 1 h 10 min after leaving DFW. There, 774 of 2,077 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were over Texas, about 2 h 45 min after leaving SEA, or 40 min after leaving DFW. There, 537 of 1,229 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is SEA to DFW a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the SEA–DFW route filed 18,993 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 81 of 18,993 (0.4%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between SEA and DFW?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, near Colorado Springs, about 2 h 15 min after leaving SEA, or 1 h 10 min after leaving DFW. The next most were over Texas, about 2 h 45 min after leaving SEA, or 40 min after leaving DFW.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly SEA to DFW?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in April and July and a bigger share in December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is DFW to SEA the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from SEA to DFW usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at SEA and DFW?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. SEA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 48 days in the past year, most often in December (18 days). DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
