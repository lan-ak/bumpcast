# LAX to SJC turbulence

> Los Angeles (LAX) ⇄ San Jose (SJC), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Los Angeles (LAX) and San Jose (SJC) fly about 310 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near LAX, and more often in December, January and March.

- Distance: 310 miles (500 km)
- In the air: about 55 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 657
- Severe reports: 12 (1.8%)
- Gusty days: LAX 17, SJC 33

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near LAX, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving LAX, or 50 min after leaving SJC. There, 75 of 158 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air.

## Questions

### Is LAX to SJC a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the LAX–SJC route filed 657 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 12 of 657 (1.8%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between LAX and SJC?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often near LAX, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving LAX, or 50 min after leaving SJC.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly LAX to SJC?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in February, April and August and a bigger share in December, January and March. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SJC to LAX the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at LAX and SJC?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. LAX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 17 days in the past year, most often in February (5 days). SJC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 33 days in the past year, most often in December (5 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
