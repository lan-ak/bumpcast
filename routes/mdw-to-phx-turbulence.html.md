# MDW to PHX turbulence

> Chicago (MDW) ⇄ Phoenix (PHX), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Chicago (MDW) and Phoenix (PHX) fly about 1,440 miles, roughly 3 hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, and more often in May.

- Distance: 1,440 miles (2,320 km)
- In the air: about 3 h 5 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 19,068
- Severe reports: 88 (0.5%)
- Gusty days: MDW 130, PHX 68

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Colorado, about 1 h 50 min after leaving MDW, or 1 h 10 min after leaving PHX. There, 629 of 1,344 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were near MDW, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 10 min after leaving MDW, or 2 h 55 min after leaving PHX. There, 545 of 2,975 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air, and gusty days at MDW add to it.

## Questions

### Is MDW to PHX a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the MDW–PHX route filed 19,068 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 88 of 19,068 (0.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between MDW and PHX?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, about 1 h 50 min after leaving MDW, or 1 h 10 min after leaving PHX. The next most were near MDW, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 10 min after leaving MDW, or 2 h 55 min after leaving PHX.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly MDW to PHX?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July and August and a bigger share in May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is PHX to MDW the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from PHX to MDW usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at MDW and PHX?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. MDW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 130 days in the past year, most often in April (20 days). PHX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 68 days in the past year, most often in June (13 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
