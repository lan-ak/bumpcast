> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) fly about 1,200 miles, roughly 2½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Indiana, and more often in April.

## Questions

### Is YYZ to DFW a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–DFW route filed 20,003 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 74 of 20,003 (0.4%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and DFW?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Indiana, near Indianapolis, about 1 h after leaving YYZ, or 1 h 35 min after leaving DFW. The next most were over Michigan, near Detroit, about 30 min after leaving YYZ, or 2 h 5 min after leaving DFW.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to DFW?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July and September and a bigger share in April. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is DFW to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from DFW to YYZ usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and DFW?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
