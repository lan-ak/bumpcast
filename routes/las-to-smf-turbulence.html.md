# LAS to SMF turbulence

> Las Vegas (LAS) ⇄ Sacramento (SMF), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Las Vegas (LAS) and Sacramento (SMF) fly about 400 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near LAS, and more often in December, April and June.

- Distance: 400 miles (640 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 5 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 622
- Severe reports: 3 (0.5%)
- Gusty days: LAS 109, SMF 33

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near LAS, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving LAS, or 1 h after leaving SMF. There, 37 of 145 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air, and gusty days at LAS add to it.

The next most were over California, near Fresno, about 35 min after leaving LAS, or 25 min after leaving SMF. There, 27 of 41 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

## Questions

### Is LAS to SMF a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the LAS–SMF route filed 622 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 3 of 622 (0.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between LAS and SMF?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often near LAS, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving LAS, or 1 h after leaving SMF. The next most were over California, near Fresno, about 35 min after leaving LAS, or 25 min after leaving SMF.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly LAS to SMF?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in May, August and September and a bigger share in December, April and June. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SMF to LAS the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at LAS and SMF?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. LAS had gusts of 25 knots or more on 109 days in the past year, most often in April (17 days). SMF had gusts of 25 knots or more on 33 days in the past year, most often in May (7 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
