# YYZ to YEG turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Edmonton (YEG), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Edmonton (YEG) fly about 1,670 miles, roughly 3½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Ontario, and more often in March, June and August.

- Distance: 1,670 miles (2,690 km)
- In the air: about 3 h 30 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,054
- Severe reports: 5 (0.5%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, YEG 63

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Ontario, about 30 min after leaving YYZ, or 3 h after leaving YEG. There, 78 of 257 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is YYZ to YEG a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–YEG route filed 1,054 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 5 of 1,054 (0.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and YEG?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Ontario, about 30 min after leaving YYZ, or 3 h after leaving YEG. The next most were over Michigan, about 1 h after leaving YYZ, or 2 h 30 min after leaving YEG.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to YEG?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in January, February and October and a bigger share in March, June and August. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is YEG to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from YEG to YYZ usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and YEG?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). YEG had gusts of 25 knots or more on 63 days in the past year, most often in May (10 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
