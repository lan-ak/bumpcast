# DEN to JAC turbulence

> Denver (DEN) ⇄ Jackson (JAC), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Denver (DEN) and Jackson (JAC) fly about 410 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near DEN, and more often in December and June.

- Distance: 410 miles (650 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 5 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 9,487
- Severe reports: 74 (0.8%)
- Gusty days: DEN 155, JAC 93

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near DEN, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving DEN, or 1 h after leaving JAC. There, 598 of 3,065 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over the mountains stirs the air, and gusty days at DEN add to it.

## Takeoff and landing

DEN had gusts of 25 knots or more on 155 days in the past year, most often in June (22 days). JAC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 93 days in the past year, most often in March (14 days).

## Questions

### Is DEN to JAC a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the DEN–JAC route filed 9,487 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 74 of 9,487 (0.8%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between DEN and JAC?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often near DEN, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving DEN, or 1 h after leaving JAC.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly DEN to JAC?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, September and October and a bigger share in December and June. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is JAC to DEN the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at DEN and JAC?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. DEN had gusts of 25 knots or more on 155 days in the past year, most often in June (22 days). JAC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 93 days in the past year, most often in March (14 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
