# SEA to SFO turbulence

> Seattle (SEA) ⇄ San Francisco (SFO), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Seattle (SEA) and San Francisco (SFO) fly about 680 miles, roughly 1½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near SEA, and more often in November and December. Severe reports were rare: 4 of 2,463.

- Distance: 680 miles (1,090 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 35 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 2,463
- Severe reports: 4 (0.2%)
- Gusty days: SEA 48, SFO 144

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near SEA, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving SEA (1 h 30 min after leaving SFO): 152 of 506 reports there. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air.

The next most were over Oregon, near Portland, about 30 min after leaving SEA (1 h after leaving SFO): 107 of 200 reports there. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is SEA to SFO a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the SEA–SFO route filed 2,463 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 4 of 2,463 (0.2%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between SEA and SFO?

Moderate bumps were reported most often near SEA, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving SEA (1 h 30 min after leaving SFO): 152 of 506 reports there. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air. The next most were over Oregon, near Portland, about 30 min after leaving SEA (1 h after leaving SFO): 107 of 200 reports there. ### When is the smoothest time of year to fly SEA to SFO?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in March and a bigger share in November and December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SFO to SEA the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at SEA and SFO?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. SEA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 48 days in the past year, most often in December (18 days). SFO had gusts of 25 knots or more on 144 days in the past year, most often in July (24 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
