# YYZ to JFK turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ New York (JFK), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and New York (JFK) fly about 370 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over New York, and more often in November and December.

- Distance: 370 miles (590 km)
- In the air: about 1 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 4,440
- Severe reports: 37 (0.8%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, JFK 157

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over New York, near Syracuse, about 25 min after leaving YYZ, or 30 min after leaving JFK. There, 269 of 1,024 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Takeoff and landing

YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). JFK had gusts of 25 knots or more on 157 days in the past year, most often in December (20 days).

## Questions

### Is YYZ to JFK a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–JFK route filed 4,440 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 37 of 4,440 (0.8%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and JFK?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over New York, near Syracuse, about 25 min after leaving YYZ, or 30 min after leaving JFK.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to JFK?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July and September and a bigger share in November and December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is JFK to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and JFK?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). JFK had gusts of 25 knots or more on 157 days in the past year, most often in December (20 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
