# MCO to PHL turbulence

> Orlando (MCO) ⇄ Philadelphia (PHL), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Orlando (MCO) and Philadelphia (PHL) fly about 860 miles, roughly 2 hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Virginia, and more often in November, December and February.

- Distance: 860 miles (1,390 km)
- In the air: about 2 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 8,403
- Severe reports: 75 (0.9%)
- Gusty days: MCO 61, PHL 118

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Virginia, near Richmond, about 1 h 25 min after leaving MCO, or 30 min after leaving PHL. There, 610 of 908 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is MCO to PHL a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the MCO–PHL route filed 8,403 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 75 of 8,403 (0.9%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between MCO and PHL?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Virginia, near Richmond, about 1 h 25 min after leaving MCO, or 30 min after leaving PHL.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly MCO to PHL?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in November, December and February. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is PHL to MCO the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at MCO and PHL?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. MCO had gusts of 25 knots or more on 61 days in the past year, most often in October (9 days). PHL had gusts of 25 knots or more on 118 days in the past year, most often in May (17 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
