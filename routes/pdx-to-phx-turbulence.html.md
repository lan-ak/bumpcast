# PDX to PHX turbulence

> Portland (PDX) ⇄ Phoenix (PHX), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Portland (PDX) and Phoenix (PHX) fly about 1,010 miles, roughly 2¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Oregon, and more often in December.

- Distance: 1,010 miles (1,630 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 15 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 2,350
- Severe reports: 5 (0.2%)
- Gusty days: PDX 40, PHX 68

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Oregon, about 20 min after leaving PDX, or 1 h 50 min after leaving PHX. There, 128 of 222 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

The next most were over Nevada, about 55 min after leaving PDX, or 1 h 15 min after leaving PHX. There, 123 of 205 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

## Questions

### Is PDX to PHX a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the PDX–PHX route filed 2,350 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 5 of 2,350 (0.2%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between PDX and PHX?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Oregon, about 20 min after leaving PDX, or 1 h 50 min after leaving PHX. The next most were over Nevada, about 55 min after leaving PDX, or 1 h 15 min after leaving PHX.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly PDX to PHX?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July and August and a bigger share in December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is PHX to PDX the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from PDX to PHX usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at PDX and PHX?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. PDX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 40 days in the past year, most often in December (9 days). PHX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 68 days in the past year, most often in June (13 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
