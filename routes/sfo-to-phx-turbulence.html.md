# SFO to PHX turbulence

> San Francisco (SFO) ⇄ Phoenix (PHX), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between San Francisco (SFO) and Phoenix (PHX) fly about 650 miles, roughly 1½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over California, and more often in December, March and October.

- Distance: 650 miles (1,050 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 35 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,130
- Severe reports: 10 (0.9%)
- Gusty days: SFO 144, PHX 68

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over California, near San Jose, about 15 min after leaving SFO, or 1 h 15 min after leaving PHX. There, 79 of 134 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is SFO to PHX a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the SFO–PHX route filed 1,130 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 10 of 1,130 (0.9%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between SFO and PHX?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over California, near San Jose, about 15 min after leaving SFO, or 1 h 15 min after leaving PHX.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly SFO to PHX?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in December, March and October. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is PHX to SFO the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from SFO to PHX usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at SFO and PHX?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. SFO had gusts of 25 knots or more on 144 days in the past year, most often in July (24 days). PHX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 68 days in the past year, most often in June (13 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
