# YYZ to MCO turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Orlando (MCO), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Orlando (MCO) fly about 1,060 miles, roughly 2¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, and more often in December and April.

- Distance: 1,060 miles (1,700 km)

## Questions

### Is YYZ to MCO a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–MCO route filed 14,953 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 51 of 14,953 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and MCO?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, near Pittsburgh, about 25 min after leaving YYZ, or 1 h 50 min after leaving MCO. The next most were over Virginia, near Greensboro, about 1 h after leaving YYZ, or 1 h 20 min after leaving MCO.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to MCO?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in December and April. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is MCO to YYZ the same?

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
