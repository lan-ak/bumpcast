# PHX to IAH turbulence

> Phoenix (PHX) ⇄ Houston (IAH), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Phoenix (PHX) and Houston (IAH) fly about 1,010 miles, roughly 2¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Texas, and more often in December, February and May.

- Distance: 1,010 miles (1,620 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 15 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 7,284
- Severe reports: 16 (0.2%)
- Gusty days: PHX 68, IAH 62

## Questions

### Is PHX to IAH a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the PHX–IAH route filed 7,284 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 16 of 7,284 (0.2%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between PHX and IAH?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Texas, near Austin, about 1 h 45 min after leaving PHX, or 25 min after leaving IAH. The next most were over New Mexico, near El Paso, about 45 min after leaving PHX, or 1 h 25 min after leaving IAH.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly PHX to IAH?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in August, September and October and a bigger share in December, February and May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is IAH to PHX the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from PHX to IAH usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at PHX and IAH?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. PHX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 68 days in the past year, most often in June (13 days). IAH had gusts of 25 knots or more on 62 days in the past year, most often in March (12 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
