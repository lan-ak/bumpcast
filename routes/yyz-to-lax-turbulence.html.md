# YYZ to LAX turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Los Angeles (LAX), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Los Angeles (LAX) fly about 2,170 miles, roughly 4½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, and more often in May and June.

- Distance: 2,170 miles (3,490 km)
- In the air: about 4 h 30 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 30,261
- Severe reports: 108 (0.4%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, LAX 17

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Colorado, near Colorado Springs, about 2 h 45 min after leaving YYZ, or 1 h 40 min after leaving LAX. There, 873 of 2,764 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were over Iowa, about 1 h 15 min after leaving YYZ, or 3 h 10 min after leaving LAX. There, 598 of 2,778 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is YYZ to LAX a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–LAX route filed 30,261 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 108 of 30,261 (0.4%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and LAX?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, near Colorado Springs, about 2 h 45 min after leaving YYZ, or 1 h 40 min after leaving LAX. The next most were over Iowa, about 1 h 15 min after leaving YYZ, or 3 h 10 min after leaving LAX.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to LAX?

Moderate bumps made up a bigger share of reports in May and June. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is LAX to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from LAX to YYZ usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and LAX?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). LAX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 17 days in the past year, most often in February (5 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
