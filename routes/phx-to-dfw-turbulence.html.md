# PHX to DFW turbulence

> Phoenix (PHX) ⇄ Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Phoenix (PHX) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) fly about 870 miles, roughly 2 hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over or near Texas, and more often in February and March.

- Distance: 870 miles (1,390 km)
- In the air: about 2 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 5,475
- Severe reports: 19 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: PHX 68, DFW 122

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over or near Texas, about 1 h 30 min after leaving PHX, or 25 min after leaving DFW. There, 199 of 962 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is PHX to DFW a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the PHX–DFW route filed 5,475 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 19 of 5,475 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between PHX and DFW?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over or near Texas, about 1 h 30 min after leaving PHX, or 25 min after leaving DFW.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly PHX to DFW?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in November, July and September and a bigger share in February and March. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is DFW to PHX the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from PHX to DFW usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at PHX and DFW?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. PHX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 68 days in the past year, most often in June (13 days). DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
