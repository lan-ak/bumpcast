# YVR to LAX turbulence

> Vancouver (YVR) ⇄ Los Angeles (LAX), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Vancouver (YVR) and Los Angeles (LAX) fly about 1,080 miles, roughly 2½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near LAX.

- Distance: 1,080 miles (1,740 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 25 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 3,097
- Severe reports: 18 (0.6%)
- Gusty days: YVR 32, LAX 17

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near LAX, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 2 h 15 min after leaving YVR, or 5 min after leaving LAX. There, 194 of 321 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air.

The next most were over Oregon, about 1 h 5 min after leaving YVR, or 1 h 15 min after leaving LAX. There, 163 of 292 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

## Questions

### Is YVR to LAX a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YVR–LAX route filed 3,097 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 18 of 3,097 (0.6%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between YVR and LAX?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often near LAX, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 2 h 15 min after leaving YVR, or 5 min after leaving LAX. The next most were over Oregon, about 1 h 5 min after leaving YVR, or 1 h 15 min after leaving LAX.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YVR to LAX?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in March and July. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is LAX to YVR the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YVR and LAX?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YVR had gusts of 25 knots or more on 32 days in the past year, most often in December (8 days). LAX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 17 days in the past year, most often in February (5 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
