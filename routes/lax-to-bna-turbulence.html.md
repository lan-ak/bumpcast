# LAX to BNA turbulence

> Los Angeles (LAX) ⇄ Nashville (BNA), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Los Angeles (LAX) and Nashville (BNA) fly about 1,790 miles, roughly 3¾ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over New Mexico, and more often in March and May.

- Distance: 1,790 miles (2,890 km)
- In the air: about 3 h 45 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 17,619
- Severe reports: 77 (0.4%)
- Gusty days: LAX 17, BNA 97

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over New Mexico, about 1 h 35 min after leaving LAX, or 2 h 5 min after leaving BNA. There, 749 of 1,592 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were over Tennessee, near Memphis, about 3 h 20 min after leaving LAX, or 20 min after leaving BNA. There, 625 of 1,486 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is LAX to BNA a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the LAX–BNA route filed 17,619 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 77 of 17,619 (0.4%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between LAX and BNA?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over New Mexico, about 1 h 35 min after leaving LAX, or 2 h 5 min after leaving BNA. The next most were over Tennessee, near Memphis, about 3 h 20 min after leaving LAX, or 20 min after leaving BNA.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly LAX to BNA?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in March and May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is BNA to LAX the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from LAX to BNA usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at LAX and BNA?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. LAX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 17 days in the past year, most often in February (5 days). BNA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 97 days in the past year, most often in March (17 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
