# HKG to NRT turbulence

> Hong Kong (HKG) ⇄ Tokyo (NRT), both directions. Model estimate, November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Hong Kong (HKG) and Tokyo (NRT) fly about 1,840 miles, roughly 3¾ hours in the air. Pilots file few turbulence reports on this route, so this page is a model estimate, by season. In a year of weather-model analyses, the air at cruising height was bumpier in January, February and March and calmer in July, August and September.

## Questions

### Is HKG to NRT a bumpy flight?

Most flights on any airline route see light chop at most, and strong bumps are rare. On the US routes where we can count reports, 0.4% of airliner reports at cruising height were severe. We can't count reports on HKG–NRT the same way: few are filed outside North America in the archive we use. In the model, the air at cruising height was bumpier than usual in January, February and March, and calmer in July, August and September. On the day, the weather decides.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly HKG to NRT?

In a year of weather-model analyses (November 2025 to October 2026), the air at cruising height was calmer in July, August and September and bumpier in January, February and March. Treat it as a rough guide: on US routes, the model's calmer and bumpier months matched pilot reports on 7 of the 16 routes we checked. Any month can bring a smooth or a bumpy day.

### Is NRT to HKG the same?

Yes. It's the same air, just at different times into the flight. Flights from HKG to NRT usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Why doesn't this page say where on the route it's bumpiest?

Because we couldn't check it. On US routes, where pilots file thousands of reports, the stretches the model rated bumpiest over a year didn't match the stretches where pilots reported the most bumps, so we only show months here.

### Can I get a turbulence forecast for my HKG to NRT flight?

Yes, as an approximate outlook. In BumpCast, turn on Global outlooks (beta) in Settings for a turbulence outlook on international flights like HKG–NRT, from the same two weather models. Within the continental US and southern Canada, the app's forecasts are more detailed. It's free to download on the App Store for iPhone.

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. 1,460 analyses, four a day, from ECMWF's open data and NOAA's GFS, the two global weather models behind the app's Global outlooks.
- Turbulence: BumpCast's clear-air turbulence estimate from each model's winds and temperatures, averaged over the two models, read every 5 minutes along a typical flight (direct path, typical climb, cruise and descent).
- Light bumps or more: an estimate of 0.10 or more (eddy dissipation rate). Each month is compared with the route's own average.
- Checked against 441,678 airliner pilot reports over the US and Canada: real skill report by report (https://bumpcast.app/learn/forecast-accuracy.html), but over a year the model's months matched pilot reports on 7 of 16 US routes and its bumpiest stretches on 2. So these pages show months only, as a rough guide.
- Weather models miss most bumps near thunderstorms and over small hills. Contains ECMWF open data (CC BY 4.0). GFS data from NOAA.
