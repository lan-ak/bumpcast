# SEA to SJC turbulence

> Seattle (SEA) ⇄ San Jose (SJC), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Seattle (SEA) and San Jose (SJC) fly about 700 miles, roughly 1¾ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near SEA, and more often in December.

- Distance: 700 miles (1,120 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 40 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 2,734
- Severe reports: 9 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: SEA 48, SJC 33

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near SEA, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving SEA, or 1 h 35 min after leaving SJC. There, 154 of 512 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air.

The next most were over Oregon, near Portland, about 30 min after leaving SEA, or 1 h after leaving SJC. There, 145 of 261 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is SEA to SJC a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the SEA–SJC route filed 2,734 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 9 of 2,734 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between SEA and SJC?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often near SEA, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving SEA, or 1 h 35 min after leaving SJC. The next most were over Oregon, near Portland, about 30 min after leaving SEA, or 1 h after leaving SJC.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly SEA to SJC?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in March and a bigger share in December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SJC to SEA the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at SEA and SJC?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. SEA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 48 days in the past year, most often in December (18 days). SJC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 33 days in the past year, most often in December (5 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
