# YYZ to TPA turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Tampa (TPA), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Tampa (TPA) fly about 1,100 miles, roughly 2½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, and more often in December, February and April.

- Distance: 1,100 miles (1,760 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 25 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 22,338
- Severe reports: 71 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, TPA 45

## Questions

### Is YYZ to TPA a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–TPA route filed 22,338 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 71 of 22,338 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and TPA?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, near Pittsburgh, about 30 min after leaving YYZ, or 1 h 55 min after leaving TPA. The next most were over West Virginia, about 50 min after leaving YYZ, or 1 h 35 min after leaving TPA.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to TPA?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in December, February and April. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is TPA to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and TPA?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). TPA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 45 days in the past year, most often in February (6 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
