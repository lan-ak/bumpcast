Status inside the route header, the forecast first

Flight trackers show a delay inside the route row: the new time next to the old one, struck through, and "2 min late" in the middle. This revision borrows that pattern without the tracker look. Times sit beside the airport codes, the status is one small word under the plane, and there's no extra line, so the header is no taller than before. The forecast is the first card again. There's no red or green, no "Arrived" badge, and no alarm icons.

The proposal
  • The route header carries the times: "EWR 2:35 PM ✈ YYZ 4:15 PM". The "Flight status" card goes away.
  • A delay shows in place: the new time in bold, with the old time struck through in grey beneath it, on both sides.
  • No extra line. The status is a small label under the plane: "On time", "40 min late", "Departed", "Diverted", "Cancelled", "Checking…". A delay is teal, diverted and cancelled are bold ink, and everything else is quiet. Severity colours are never used.
  • Gate only when there is one. "Gate B22 · T5" appears under the departure time once a gate number is set. A terminal on its own ("Terminal A", no gate) isn't shown at a glance; it's in the details.
  • Tap the header to show details inline: scheduled, expected and actual times, gate and terminal, and "Updated 4:59 PM · next update around 10:59 PM".
  • Details open automatically, once, the first time you look at the flight after a change (a new delay, gate, cancellation or diversion). After that the line goes back to closed. When the status is more than 2 h old, the label adds "updated 3 h ago" underneath.
  • The forecast note moves into the forecast card: "Updated for the new 7:55 AM departure."
  • Still automatic, and fresher near departure (approved): status refreshes every 6 h from 48 to 18 h out, hourly from 18 h out, and every 15 min in the last 3 h before departure until about an hour after. Each check updates the expected and actual departure and arrival times and the gate. If the departure moves, the forecast is recomputed for the new time in the same step, including the time in the air. The delay push is unchanged ("Delayed 40 min, forecast updated").
  • Unchanged: the cancelled card replaces the forecast, and the diverted card sits above the faded forecast. We don't track landings, so there's no "Arrived" state.
  • Small fix: the delay icon in the list row changes from clock.badge.exclamationmark to clock.
