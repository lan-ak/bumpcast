Forecasts beyond the US, honest about how sure they are

The global outlook is less reliable than a confirmed forecast. Over the US it missed about two-thirds of moderate pilot reports, against a third for the confirmed forecast. For a nervous flyer, the worst outcome is "calm" followed by a bumpy flight. So outlook stretches look and read rougher: a beta label, no score, approximate times, ranges instead of one level, and never "smooth". Official warnings are the most credible signal outside the US, so they are prominent. The full copy is in docs/design/global-forecasts-copy.md. Approved by Lanre on revision 2.

Trust rules for outlook stretches (outlook-only routes, and the outlook parts of partly confirmed ones)
  1. Label: "Global outlook (beta)". The first rollout is opt-in for waitlist users.
  2. No numeric score. Grade only.
  3. Approximate times: "about 3 h in, for around 40 min". The chart draws outlook stretches as coarse 30-minute blocks.
  4. Ranges, not one level: "Light to moderate possible".
  5. Never "smooth". Instead: "No major bumps expected", with a standing line, "Light bumps are possible on any flight."
  6. Official warnings are prominent: a dark banner above the chart and a solid marker on it, e.g. "Official warning: thunderstorms over Recife".
  7. Always ask for a rating after these flights land.
  8. Paywall: never implies the outlook matches US quality. Two options are below.
  9. Opt-in: from the waitlist card, Settings, or when adding an international flight.
Decisions (approved)
  1. Paywall: option B. The global outlook (beta) is included in Pro, with plain copy that says exactly what you get (section 5).
  2. "Based on" line: kept: "worldwide weather forecasts, checked against official warnings and recent pilot reports".
  3. Step 1 coverage line: unchanged: "We have the strongest coverage in the US, and we're expanding rapidly."

2 · Forecast screen

Warnings up front, outlook stretches coarse

An official warning gets a dark banner above the chart and a solid marker over its stretch; tap either for the details. Confirmed stretches keep exact minute bars and times. Outlook stretches are drawn as faded 30-minute blocks, and their rows give ranges and approximate times. There's no score on these screens.
