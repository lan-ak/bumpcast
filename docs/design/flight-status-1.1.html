Flight-number search and live status

Search by flight number is free. Live status is Pro, or comes with a flight pass for that flight. It refreshes with each forecast update: every 6 h from 48 h to 18 h out, then hourly until about an hour after departure. When the departure time moves, the forecast is recomputed in the same step. Full copy is in docs/design/flight-status-1.1-copy.md.

1 · Add a flight · free

Find your flight by number

This becomes the first screen of the add-flight sheet. "Choose airports instead" leads to the existing route step, which still works for everything. A match skips straight to Check your flight.

1a · Add a flight by number · schedule not out yet

A flight booked before its schedule is published

There's no search horizon of our own; search goes as far ahead as airlines have published schedules, usually 9–11 months. A number further out than that isn't a dead end. We explain calmly, the user enters airports and time from their booking, and the number stays with the flight. When the airline publishes the schedule, the app fills in the times. If they differ, the forecast uses the airline's times. The route-list "aren't out yet" state in 1b stays for route searches.

1b · Add a flight by airports · free

Flights on this route

This sits behind "Choose airports instead". Once From, To and the day are set, the When step lists that day's nonstop flights, operating carrier only and sorted by departure. Tapping a row goes to Check your flight, the same as a number match, so the flight has a number and gets live status on Pro. Busy routes are grouped into morning, afternoon and evening, with chips to jump between them. Every state keeps Enter the time myself, which opens today's time wheel.

1c · Add a connecting flight · free

The next flight of the same trip

On Check your flight, a secondary action sits next to the primary save. It saves this flight, then reopens the sheet for the next leg: the search starts from the arrival airport, the day defaults to the arrival date (which matters for overnight arrivals), and times default to after the arrival. A context line keeps it clear this is the same trip. Each flight still saves and forecasts on its own. Trip grouping, linked cards and connection-risk alerts are planned for 1.2.

2 · Flights list

Flight number and a compact status row

The number comes before the route. With Pro or a flight pass, a status row sits under the date. Free users see a locked row that opens the live_status_locked placement. Status uses ink and teal only, so it never looks like a turbulence level.

3 · Flight detail

Status section, with a forecast that moves with you

This section sits above the forecast. Times are in a scheduled, expected and actual grid, and the freshness line is always visible. When the departure time moves, a note explains that the forecast moved too.

4 · Notifications

One message per real change

These follow the same quiet hours as forecast updates: nothing from 22:00 to 07:00 local time unless the flight leaves within 4 h. A delay and the forecast change it causes always arrive together. Departure moves under 15 min stay in the app. Gate changes never push, because gates refresh at most hourly and a late push would mislead; the gate shows on the card instead.
