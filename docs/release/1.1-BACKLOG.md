# BumpCast 1.1 backlog

Things decided for after 1.0 ships. Newest first.

## Arm the in-flight countdown before boarding (sells the Flight Pass at the gate)

**Problem.** The "Boarding soon?" card appears 1 hour before departure, but its copy says "Tap when you take off". A free user who taps "We've taken off" hits the `live_activity_locked` paywall in the air, usually with no internet:
- Superwall may show a cached paywall, but StoreKit can't complete a purchase offline;
- so the sale is lost and they get no countdown.

This is probably the Flight Pass's best moment, so it should happen on the ground.

**Change.**
- **Free users, on the boarding card:** new copy along the lines of "Get your bump countdown ready before you board". The button opens the `live_activity_locked` paywall at the gate, with the Flight Pass for this flight as the lead offer.
- **Pro and passholders: "arm" the flight on the ground.**
  - The Live Activity starts at the scheduled departure, or the live departure time when flight status has it.
  - "We've taken off" stays as a manual correction for the real wheels-up time.
  - The stretch reminders are rescheduled from that.
- **Offline fallback:** if a free user still taps in the air with no connection, show a calm note instead of a paywall. Something like: "Connect to wifi to unlock the countdown, or get it ready before your next flight."
- **Measure:** compare Flight Pass purchases from `live_activity_locked` by hours to departure, before and after.

**Where:**
- `ios/BumpCast/Views/FlightCards.swift`: the `InFlightCard` boarding state;
- `ios/BumpCast/Services/InFlight.swift`: `canTakeOff` and `takeOff`, plus an armed state that starts the Live Activity at departure;
- `ios/BumpCast/Services/Paywalls.swift`: the `unlock` reachability check;
- Superwall headline copy for `live_activity_locked`, in `superwall/messages/en.ts`.

## "Rate BumpCast" row in Settings

A user-tapped row that opens `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6824269447?action=write-review`, near "Restore purchases" in `ios/BumpCast/Views/SettingsView.swift`.
- It's allowed at any time because the user starts it.
- It catches people who never saw the automatic prompt.

Keep the existing automatic prompt exactly as it is:
- Apple's `requestReview()`;
- shown after a landed flight where the forecast matched;
- at most once per version.

No custom "Do you like BumpCast?" pre-prompt, and no incentives (guideline 5.1.1 / 5.6.1).
