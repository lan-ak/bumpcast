### Description (the pitch)

This is what's in the App Store Connect draft. Apple's limit is 1,000 characters, and this is about 900.

> BumpCast forecasts the turbulence on your exact flight, minute by minute, and tells nervous flyers in calm, plain words when the bumps will come, how strong they'll be and when they'll end. Version 1.0.4 is built for iPhone Duo.
>
> Open the Duo and BumpCast becomes a spread: your flight on one side, its forecast on the other, and the turbulence timeline across both halves, so the whole flight reads at a glance. Nothing ever sits on the fold; partly open, each side stacks on its own and expands smoothly as you open it. The map, Learn, Calm and guided breathing each get two panes. Closed, it's the familiar iPhone app. Every screen was audited open, partly open and closed, at large text sizes and offline.
>
> No ads, no account, no scare graphics: breathing paced to the next bumpy stretch and a Live Activity countdown that works in airplane mode. Made by Akinyemi & Co Ltd, an independent developer.

Every fact comes from the Duo layout rules and the release ledger; nothing is invented.
