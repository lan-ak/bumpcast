Support

How can we help?

Questions, a forecast that felt wrong, or a country you'd like us to cover? Email support@akinyemi.ca. We usually reply within a day.

Which flights can BumpCast forecast?

Flights within the continental United States and southern Canada. For other routes the app says so, and you can tap "Notify me" to hear when we cover your country.

When does my forecast appear?

48 hours before departure. It updates at 24, 6 and 2 hours out, and becomes a "Confirmed forecast" on the day you fly.

How do I track my flight?

Tap +, type your flight number (like AC 1504) and pick the day, or choose your airports and pick from that day's flights. Live status, gate changes and delay alerts are free for every flight you add.

Does it work in airplane mode?

Yes. Open the app once before you board and your forecast, timeline and in-flight reminders are saved on your phone.

How accurate is it?

After you land, the app asks how bumpy it felt. We compare every answer with the forecast and show the results in Settings.

How do I add flights from my email?

Coming soon. In the add-flight screen, tap "Find flights in your email" and connect Gmail or Outlook. BumpCast reads booking emails on your iPhone only and adds the flights it finds. See how email access works.

A flight from my email is wrong or isn't mine

Open the flight and tap "Not my flight", or "Undo" if it was just added. BumpCast won't add it again.

Disconnect my email

In the app, open Settings → Email → Disconnect. You can also remove BumpCast from your Google Account or Microsoft account.

Restore or cancel a purchase

To restore BumpCast Pro on a new phone, open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases". To cancel a subscription, open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Delete my data

Email us and we'll delete everything linked to your device within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.
