# YYZ to EWR turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Newark (EWR), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Newark (EWR) fly about 350 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over New York, and more often in December and October.

- Distance: 350 miles (560 km)
- In the air: about 1 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 3,314
- Severe reports: 29 (0.9%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, EWR 145

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over New York, near Syracuse, about 30 min after leaving YYZ, or 25 min after leaving EWR. There, 139 of 367 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

The next most were near EWR, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 55 min after leaving YYZ, or 5 min after leaving EWR. There, 126 of 267 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air, and gusty days at EWR add to it.

## Takeoff and landing

YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). EWR had gusts of 25 knots or more on 145 days in the past year, most often in December (19 days).

## Questions

### Is YYZ to EWR a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–EWR route filed 3,314 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 29 of 3,314 (0.9%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and EWR?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over New York, near Syracuse, about 30 min after leaving YYZ, or 25 min after leaving EWR. The next most were near EWR, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 55 min after leaving YYZ, or 5 min after leaving EWR.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to EWR?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July and September and a bigger share in December and October. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is EWR to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and EWR?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). EWR had gusts of 25 knots or more on 145 days in the past year, most often in December (19 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
