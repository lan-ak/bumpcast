# YUL to BOS turbulence

> Montréal (YUL) ⇄ Boston (BOS), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Montréal (YUL) and Boston (BOS) fly about 250 miles, roughly 50 minutes in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Vermont, and more often in December and July.

- Distance: 250 miles (410 km)
- In the air: about 50 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,370
- Severe reports: 12 (0.9%)
- Gusty days: YUL 88, BOS 148

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Vermont, near Montréal, about 10 min after leaving YUL, or 30 min after leaving BOS. There, 38 of 116 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

The next most were near BOS, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 45 min after leaving YUL, or 5 min after leaving BOS. There, 38 of 168 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air, and gusty days at BOS add to it.

## Questions

### Is YUL to BOS a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YUL–BOS route filed 1,370 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 12 of 1,370 (0.9%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YUL and BOS?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Vermont, near Montréal, about 10 min after leaving YUL, or 30 min after leaving BOS. The next most were near BOS, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 45 min after leaving YUL, or 5 min after leaving BOS.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YUL to BOS?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in February, March and April and a bigger share in December and July. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is BOS to YUL the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YUL and BOS?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YUL had gusts of 25 knots or more on 88 days in the past year, most often in December (13 days). BOS had gusts of 25 knots or more on 148 days in the past year, most often in December (23 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
