# LAS to SLC turbulence

> Las Vegas (LAS) ⇄ Salt Lake City (SLC), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Las Vegas (LAS) and Salt Lake City (SLC) fly about 370 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Utah, and more often in December, May and June.

- Distance: 370 miles (590 km)
- In the air: about 1 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,200
- Severe reports: 4 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: LAS 109, SLC 105

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Utah, near Salt Lake City, about 50 min after leaving LAS, or 5 min after leaving SLC. There, 80 of 176 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over the mountains stirs the air, and gusty days at SLC add to it.

The next most were over or near Utah, about 25 min after leaving LAS, or 30 min after leaving SLC. There, 57 of 80 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

## Takeoff and landing

LAS had gusts of 25 knots or more on 109 days in the past year, most often in April (17 days). SLC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 105 days in the past year, most often in July (14 days).

## Questions

### Is LAS to SLC a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the LAS–SLC route filed 1,200 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 4 of 1,200 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between LAS and SLC?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Utah, near Salt Lake City, about 50 min after leaving LAS, or 5 min after leaving SLC. The next most were over or near Utah, about 25 min after leaving LAS, or 30 min after leaving SLC.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly LAS to SLC?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in November, July and August and a bigger share in December, May and June. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SLC to LAS the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at LAS and SLC?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. LAS had gusts of 25 knots or more on 109 days in the past year, most often in April (17 days). SLC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 105 days in the past year, most often in July (14 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
