# YHZ to YUL turbulence

> Halifax (YHZ) ⇄ Montréal (YUL), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Halifax (YHZ) and Montréal (YUL) fly about 500 miles, roughly 1¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Maine, and more often in December, May and October.

- Distance: 500 miles (800 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 15 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,197
- Severe reports: 6 (0.5%)
- Gusty days: YHZ 135, YUL 88

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Maine, about 35 min after leaving YHZ, or 35 min after leaving YUL. There, 122 of 266 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

Few pilot reports are filed over Nova Scotia, New Brunswick and Quebec in the archive these pages use, so those stretches have little data. Few reports there doesn't mean smooth air.

## Questions

### Is YHZ to YUL a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YHZ–YUL route filed 1,197 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 6 of 1,197 (0.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YHZ and YUL?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Maine, about 35 min after leaving YHZ, or 35 min after leaving YUL.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YHZ to YUL?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in November, January and June and a bigger share in December, May and October. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is YUL to YHZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from YUL to YHZ usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YHZ and YUL?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YHZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 135 days in the past year, most often in December (19 days). YUL had gusts of 25 knots or more on 88 days in the past year, most often in December (13 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
