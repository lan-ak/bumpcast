# YYC to SFO turbulence

> Calgary (YYC) ⇄ San Francisco (SFO), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Calgary (YYC) and San Francisco (SFO) fly about 1,020 miles, roughly 2¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over California.

- Distance: 1,020 miles (1,640 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 15 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 2,091
- Severe reports: 14 (0.7%)
- Gusty days: YYC 121, SFO 144

## Questions

### Is YYC to SFO a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYC–SFO route filed 2,091 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 14 of 2,091 (0.7%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYC to SFO?

### Is SFO to YYC the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from SFO to YYC usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYC and SFO?

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
