# YUL to ORD turbulence

> Montréal (YUL) ⇄ Chicago (ORD), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Montréal (YUL) and Chicago (ORD) fly about 750 miles, roughly 1¾ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Michigan, and more often in March, April and May.

- Distance: 750 miles (1,200 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 45 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 5,767
- Severe reports: 28 (0.5%)
- Gusty days: YUL 88, ORD 123

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Michigan, near Grand Rapids, about 1 h 10 min after leaving YUL, or 30 min after leaving ORD. There, 299 of 613 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

The next most were near ORD, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 1 h 40 min after leaving YUL, or 5 min after leaving ORD. There, 238 of 1,659 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air, and gusty days at ORD add to it.

Few pilot reports are filed over Ontario in the archive these pages use, so those stretches have little data. Few reports there doesn't mean smooth air.

## Questions

### Is YUL to ORD a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YUL–ORD route filed 5,767 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 28 of 5,767 (0.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between YUL and ORD?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Michigan, near Grand Rapids, about 1 h 10 min after leaving YUL, or 30 min after leaving ORD. The next most were near ORD, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 1 h 40 min after leaving YUL, or 5 min after leaving ORD.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YUL to ORD?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in December, February and July and a bigger share in March, April and May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is ORD to YUL the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from ORD to YUL usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YUL and ORD?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YUL had gusts of 25 knots or more on 88 days in the past year, most often in December (13 days). ORD had gusts of 25 knots or more on 123 days in the past year, most often in April (20 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
