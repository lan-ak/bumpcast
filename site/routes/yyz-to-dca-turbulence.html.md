# YYZ to DCA turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Washington (DCA), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Washington (DCA) fly about 360 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania.

- Distance: 360 miles (580 km)
- In the air: about 1 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 2,638
- Severe reports: 33 (1.3%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, DCA 99

## Questions

### Is YYZ to DCA a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–DCA route filed 2,638 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 33 of 2,638 (1.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and DCA?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, about 25 min after leaving YYZ, or 30 min after leaving DCA. The next most were over New York, near Buffalo, about 15 min after leaving YYZ, or 40 min after leaving DCA.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to DCA?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is DCA to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and DCA?

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
