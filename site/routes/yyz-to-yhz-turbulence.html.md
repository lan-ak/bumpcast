## Questions

### Is YYZ to YHZ a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–YHZ route filed 3,374 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 27 of 3,374 (0.8%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Maine, about 1 h 10 min after leaving YYZ, or 35 min after leaving YHZ. The next most were over Vermont, near Montréal, about 45 min after leaving YYZ, or 1 h after leaving YHZ.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to YHZ?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in January and February and a bigger share in December and October. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is YHZ to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from YYZ to YHZ usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
