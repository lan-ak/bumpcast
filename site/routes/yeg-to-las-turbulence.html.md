# YEG to LAS turbulence

> Edmonton (YEG) ⇄ Las Vegas (LAS), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Edmonton (YEG) and Las Vegas (LAS) fly about 1,190 miles, roughly 2½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Nevada, and more often in December.

- Distance: 1,190 miles (1,920 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 35 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 2,654
- Severe reports: 20 (0.8%)
- Gusty days: YEG 63, LAS 109

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Nevada, about 1 h 50 min after leaving YEG, or 45 min after leaving LAS. There, 192 of 323 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were over or near Nevada, about 2 h 10 min after leaving YEG, or 25 min after leaving LAS. There, 148 of 214 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is YEG to LAS a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YEG–LAS route filed 2,654 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 20 of 2,654 (0.8%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between YEG and LAS?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Nevada, about 1 h 50 min after leaving YEG, or 45 min after leaving LAS. The next most were over or near Nevada, about 2 h 10 min after leaving YEG, or 25 min after leaving LAS.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YEG to LAS?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in June, July and August and a bigger share in December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is LAS to YEG the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YEG and LAS?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YEG had gusts of 25 knots or more on 63 days in the past year, most often in May (10 days). LAS had gusts of 25 knots or more on 109 days in the past year, most often in April (17 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
