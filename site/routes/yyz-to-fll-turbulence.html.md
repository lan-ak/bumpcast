# YYZ to FLL turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Fort Lauderdale (FLL), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL) fly about 1,220 miles, roughly 2¾ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, and more often in November, February and April.

## Questions

### Is YYZ to FLL a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–FLL route filed 15,357 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 60 of 15,357 (0.4%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and FLL?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, near Pittsburgh, about 25 min after leaving YYZ, or 2 h 10 min after leaving FLL. The next most were over Virginia, near Greensboro, about 1 h after leaving YYZ, or 1 h 35 min after leaving FLL.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to FLL?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in November, February and April. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is FLL to YYZ the same?

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
