# SFO to SLC turbulence

> San Francisco (SFO) ⇄ Salt Lake City (SLC), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between San Francisco (SFO) and Salt Lake City (SLC) fly about 600 miles, roughly 1½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near SLC, and more often in December and September.

- Distance: 600 miles (960 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 25 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,741
- Severe reports: 12 (0.7%)
- Gusty days: SFO 144, SLC 105

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near SLC, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 1 h 20 min after leaving SFO, or 5 min after leaving SLC. There, 99 of 251 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over the mountains stirs the air, and gusty days at SLC add to it.

The next most were over Nevada, about 55 min after leaving SFO, or 30 min after leaving SLC. There, 85 of 144 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

## Questions

### Is SFO to SLC a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the SFO–SLC route filed 1,741 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 12 of 1,741 (0.7%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between SFO and SLC?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often near SLC, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 1 h 20 min after leaving SFO, or 5 min after leaving SLC. The next most were over Nevada, about 55 min after leaving SFO, or 30 min after leaving SLC.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly SFO to SLC?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in April and July and a bigger share in December and September. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SLC to SFO the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from SFO to SLC usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at SFO and SLC?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. SFO had gusts of 25 knots or more on 144 days in the past year, most often in July (24 days). SLC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 105 days in the past year, most often in July (14 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
