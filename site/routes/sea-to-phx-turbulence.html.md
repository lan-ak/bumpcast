# SEA to PHX turbulence

> Seattle (SEA) ⇄ Phoenix (PHX), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Seattle (SEA) and Phoenix (PHX) fly about 1,110 miles, roughly 2½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near SEA, and more often in December.

- Distance: 1,110 miles (1,780 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 25 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 3,301
- Severe reports: 5 (0.2%)
- Gusty days: SEA 48, PHX 68

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near SEA, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving SEA, or 2 h 20 min after leaving PHX. There, 239 of 682 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air.

The next most were over Nevada, about 1 h 25 min after leaving SEA, or 55 min after leaving PHX. There, 150 of 233 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

## Questions

### Is SEA to PHX a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the SEA–PHX route filed 3,301 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 5 of 3,301 (0.2%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between SEA and PHX?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often near SEA, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 5 min after leaving SEA, or 2 h 20 min after leaving PHX. The next most were over Nevada, about 1 h 25 min after leaving SEA, or 55 min after leaving PHX.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly SEA to PHX?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in April, July and August and a bigger share in December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is PHX to SEA the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from SEA to PHX usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at SEA and PHX?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. SEA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 48 days in the past year, most often in December (18 days). PHX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 68 days in the past year, most often in June (13 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
