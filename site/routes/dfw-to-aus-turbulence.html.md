# DFW to AUS turbulence

> Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) ⇄ Austin (AUS), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Austin (AUS) fly about 190 miles, roughly 40 minutes in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Texas, and more often in February, March and May.

- Distance: 190 miles (310 km)
- In the air: about 40 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,488
- Severe reports: 14 (0.9%)
- Gusty days: DFW 122, AUS 91

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Texas, near Dallas-Fort Worth, about 10 min after leaving DFW, or 30 min after leaving AUS. There, 89 of 196 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air, and gusty days at DFW add to it.

## Questions

### Is DFW to AUS a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the DFW–AUS route filed 1,488 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 14 of 1,488 (0.9%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between DFW and AUS?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Texas, near Dallas-Fort Worth, about 10 min after leaving DFW, or 30 min after leaving AUS.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly DFW to AUS?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in February, March and May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is AUS to DFW the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at DFW and AUS?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days). AUS had gusts of 25 knots or more on 91 days in the past year, most often in March (18 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
