# DFW to DCA turbulence

> Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) ⇄ Washington (DCA), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Washington (DCA) fly about 1,190 miles, roughly 2½ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Tennessee, and more often in March, April and May.

- Distance: 1,190 miles (1,910 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 35 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 24,716
- Severe reports: 84 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: DFW 122, DCA 99

## Questions

### Is DFW to DCA a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the DFW–DCA route filed 24,716 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 84 of 24,716 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between DFW and DCA?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Tennessee, near Nashville, about 1 h 20 min after leaving DFW, or 1 h 10 min after leaving DCA. The next most were over Kentucky, about 1 h 50 min after leaving DFW, or 45 min after leaving DCA.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly DFW to DCA?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July, August and September and a bigger share in March, April and May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is DCA to DFW the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from DFW to DCA usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at DFW and DCA?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days). DCA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 99 days in the past year, most often in March (14 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
