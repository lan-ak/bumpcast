Toronto ⇄ Philadelphia

YYZ to PHL turbulence

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Philadelphia (PHL) fly about 350 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, and more often in April.

Questions

Is YYZ to PHL a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–PHL route filed 2,402 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 35 of 2,402 (1.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and PHL?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, near Rochester, about 25 min after leaving YYZ, or 30 min after leaving PHL.

When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to PHL?

Moderate bumps made up a bigger share of reports in April. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

Is PHL to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and PHL?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). PHL had gusts of 25 knots or more on 118 days in the past year, most often in May (17 days).

How we made this page

  • Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, through the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive at Iowa State University.
  • 2,402 pilot reports of turbulence from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct YYZ–PHL path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model: a typical climb, cruise and descent). Real flights wander off the direct path, so this is the air near the route, not a record of every flight on it.
  • Airliners only: mainline and regional jets and regional turboprops, not light aircraft. Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "light to moderate" is kept separate, and "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
  • Gusty days: days with at least one hourly airport report of gusts of 25 knots or more, the same threshold the app uses for a gusty takeoff or landing.
  • What the numbers can't say: pilots don't report on every flight, and habits differ: in the past year, smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports filed with Washington Center but 45% with Miami Center. So these pages show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for your flight, and we don't rank routes against each other.
