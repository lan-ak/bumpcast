# YYZ to PHL turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Philadelphia (PHL), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Philadelphia (PHL) fly about 350 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Pennsylvania, and more often in April.

- Distance: 350 miles (560 km)
- In the air: about 1 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 2,402
- Severe reports: 35 (1.5%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, PHL 118

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Pennsylvania, near Rochester, about 25 min after leaving YYZ, or 30 min after leaving PHL. There, 188 of 457 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is YYZ to PHL a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–PHL route filed 2,402 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 35 of 2,402 (1.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
