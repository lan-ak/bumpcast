# YYZ to BOS turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Boston (BOS), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Boston (BOS) fly about 440 miles, roughly 1¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over New York, and more often in December, June and October.

- Distance: 440 miles (710 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 10 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 7,847
- Severe reports: 31 (0.4%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, BOS 148

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over New York, near Syracuse, about 30 min after leaving YYZ, or 35 min after leaving BOS. There, 326 of 1,304 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is YYZ to BOS a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–BOS route filed 7,847 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 31 of 7,847 (0.4%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YYZ and BOS?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over New York, near Syracuse, about 30 min after leaving YYZ, or 35 min after leaving BOS.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to BOS?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in November and January and a bigger share in December, June and October. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is BOS to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from YYZ to BOS usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and BOS?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). BOS had gusts of 25 knots or more on 148 days in the past year, most often in December (23 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
