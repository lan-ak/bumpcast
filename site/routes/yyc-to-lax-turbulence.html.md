# YYC to LAX turbulence

> Calgary (YYC) ⇄ Los Angeles (LAX), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Calgary (YYC) and Los Angeles (LAX) fly about 1,210 miles, roughly 2¾ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often near LAX, and more often in December.

- Distance: 1,210 miles (1,940 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 40 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 2,616
- Severe reports: 20 (0.8%)
- Gusty days: YYC 121, LAX 17

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often near LAX, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 2 h 30 min after leaving YYC, or 5 min after leaving LAX. There, 229 of 341 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air.

The next most were over Nevada, about 1 h 35 min after leaving YYC, or 1 h after leaving LAX. There, 146 of 226 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

## Questions

### Is YYC to LAX a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYC–LAX route filed 2,616 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 20 of 2,616 (0.8%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YYC and LAX?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often near LAX, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 2 h 30 min after leaving YYC, or 5 min after leaving LAX. The next most were over Nevada, about 1 h 35 min after leaving YYC, or 1 h after leaving LAX.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYC to LAX?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in April and a bigger share in December. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is LAX to YYC the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYC and LAX?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 121 days in the past year, most often in June (17 days). LAX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 17 days in the past year, most often in February (5 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
