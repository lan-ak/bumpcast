# SMF to SAN turbulence

> Sacramento (SMF) ⇄ San Diego (SAN), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Sacramento (SMF) and San Diego (SAN) fly about 480 miles, roughly 1¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over California, and more often in March.

- Distance: 480 miles (770 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 15 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,071
- Severe reports: 13 (1.2%)
- Gusty days: SMF 33, SAN 20

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over California, near Santa Ana, about 55 min after leaving SMF, or 15 min after leaving SAN. There, 59 of 75 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is SMF to SAN a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the SMF–SAN route filed 1,071 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 13 of 1,071 (1.2%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between SMF and SAN?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over California, near Santa Ana, about 55 min after leaving SMF, or 15 min after leaving SAN.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly SMF to SAN?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in June and July and a bigger share in March. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SAN to SMF the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at SMF and SAN?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. SMF had gusts of 25 knots or more on 33 days in the past year, most often in May (7 days). SAN had gusts of 25 knots or more on 20 days in the past year, most often in February (4 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
