LGA to YYZ turbulence

Flights between New York (LGA) and Toronto (YYZ) fly about 360 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over New York, and more often in December and October. Severe reports were rare: 38 of 4,788.

Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over New York, near Rochester, about 30 min after leaving LGA (25 min after leaving YYZ): 354 of 1,267 reports there. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms. More on clear-air turbulence and turbulence near clouds.

Takeoff and landing

LGA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 146 days in the past year, most often in December (20 days). YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). Questions

Is LGA to YYZ a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the LGA–YYZ route filed 4,788 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 38 of 4,788 (0.8%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

Where is it bumpiest between LGA and YYZ?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over New York, near Rochester (about 30 min after leaving LGA (25 min after leaving YYZ)).

When is the smoothest time of year to fly LGA to YYZ?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in February and July and a bigger share in December and October. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

Is YYZ to LGA the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at LGA and YYZ?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. LGA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 146 days in the past year, most often in December (20 days). YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days).

How we made this page

  • Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, through the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive at Iowa State University.
  • 4,788 pilot reports of turbulence from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct LGA–YYZ path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model: a typical climb, cruise and descent). Real flights wander off the direct path, so this is the air near the route, not a record of every flight on it.
  • Airliners only: mainline and regional jets and regional turboprops, not light aircraft. Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "light to moderate" is kept separate, and "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
  • Gusty days: days with at least one hourly airport report of gusts of 25 knots or more, the same threshold the app uses for a gusty takeoff or landing.
  • What the numbers can't say: pilots don't report on every flight, and habits differ: in the past year, smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports filed with Washington Center but 45% with Miami Center. So these pages show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for your flight, and we don't rank routes against each other.
