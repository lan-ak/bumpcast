# YOW to EWR turbulence

> Ottawa (YOW) ⇄ Newark (EWR), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Ottawa (YOW) and Newark (EWR) fly about 330 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over New York, and more often in December and October.

- Distance: 330 miles (530 km)
- In the air: about 55 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 5,069
- Severe reports: 25 (0.5%)
- Gusty days: YOW 79, EWR 145

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over New York, near Syracuse, about 25 min after leaving YOW, or 30 min after leaving EWR. There, 278 of 1,203 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is YOW to EWR a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YOW–EWR route filed 5,069 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 25 of 5,069 (0.5%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between YOW and EWR?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over New York, near Syracuse, about 25 min after leaving YOW, or 30 min after leaving EWR.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YOW to EWR?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in April and a bigger share in December and October. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is EWR to YOW the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YOW and EWR?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YOW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 79 days in the past year, most often in December (12 days). EWR had gusts of 25 knots or more on 145 days in the past year, most often in December (19 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
