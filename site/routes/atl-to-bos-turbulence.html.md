# ATL to BOS turbulence

> Atlanta (ATL) ⇄ Boston (BOS), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Atlanta (ATL) and Boston (BOS) fly about 940 miles, roughly 2¼ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Virginia, and more often in June. Severe reports were rare: 72 of 15,695.

- Distance: 940 miles (1,520 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 10 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 15,695
- Severe reports: 72 (0.5%)
- Gusty days: ATL 65, BOS 148

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly ATL to BOS?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July and a bigger share in June. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is BOS to ATL the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from ATL to BOS usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
