# DEN to EGE turbulence

> Denver (DEN) ⇄ Eagle (EGE), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Denver (DEN) and Eagle (EGE) fly about 120 miles, roughly 30 minutes in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, and more often in December, January and June.

- Distance: 120 miles (190 km)
- In the air: about 30 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 8,921
- Severe reports: 92 (1.0%)
- Gusty days: DEN 155, EGE 122

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Colorado, near Denver, about 10 min after leaving DEN, or 20 min after leaving EGE. There, 385 of 682 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were near DEN, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 0 min after leaving DEN, or 30 min after leaving EGE. There, 312 of 2,534 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over the mountains stirs the air, and gusty days at DEN add to it.

## Questions

### Is DEN to EGE a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the DEN–EGE route filed 8,921 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 92 of 8,921 (1.0%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### Where is it bumpiest between DEN and EGE?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, near Denver, about 10 min after leaving DEN, or 20 min after leaving EGE. The next most were near DEN, where flights climb out or come in to land, about 0 min after leaving DEN, or 30 min after leaving EGE.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly DEN to EGE?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in August, September and October and a bigger share in December, January and June. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is EGE to DEN the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at DEN and EGE?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. DEN had gusts of 25 knots or more on 155 days in the past year, most often in June (22 days). EGE had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in June (24 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
