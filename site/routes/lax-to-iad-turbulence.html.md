# LAX to IAD turbulence

> Los Angeles (LAX) ⇄ Dulles (IAD), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Los Angeles (LAX) and Dulles (IAD) fly about 2,280 miles, roughly 4¾ hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, and more often in December and October.

- Distance: 2,280 miles (3,670 km)
- In the air: about 4 h 45 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 30,898
- Severe reports: 106 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: LAX 17, IAD 94

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Colorado, about 1 h 45 min after leaving LAX, or 2 h 55 min after leaving IAD. There, 1,030 of 2,401 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were over Indiana, near Indianapolis, about 3 h 35 min after leaving LAX, or 1 h 5 min after leaving IAD. There, 835 of 3,506 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is LAX to IAD a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the LAX–IAD route filed 30,898 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 106 of 30,898 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between LAX and IAD?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, about 1 h 45 min after leaving LAX, or 2 h 55 min after leaving IAD. The next most were over Indiana, near Indianapolis, about 3 h 35 min after leaving LAX, or 1 h 5 min after leaving IAD.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly LAX to IAD?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July and August and a bigger share in December and October. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is IAD to LAX the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from LAX to IAD usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at LAX and IAD?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. LAX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 17 days in the past year, most often in February (5 days). IAD had gusts of 25 knots or more on 94 days in the past year, most often in March (17 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
