# LAX to SMF turbulence

> Los Angeles (LAX) ⇄ Sacramento (SMF), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Los Angeles (LAX) and Sacramento (SMF) fly about 370 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over California, and more often in December and March.

- Distance: 370 miles (600 km)
- In the air: about 1 h (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 939
- Severe reports: 10 (1.1%)
- Gusty days: LAX 17, SMF 33

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over California, near Burbank, about 5 min after leaving LAX, or 55 min after leaving SMF. There, 68 of 90 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air.

## Questions

### Is LAX to SMF a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the LAX–SMF route filed 939 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 10 of 939 (1.1%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between LAX and SMF?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over California, near Burbank, about 5 min after leaving LAX, or 55 min after leaving SMF.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly LAX to SMF?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in May, August and September and a bigger share in December and March. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SMF to LAX the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at LAX and SMF?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. LAX had gusts of 25 knots or more on 17 days in the past year, most often in February (5 days). SMF had gusts of 25 knots or more on 33 days in the past year, most often in May (7 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
