# DEN to SLC turbulence

> Denver (DEN) ⇄ Salt Lake City (SLC), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Denver (DEN) and Salt Lake City (SLC) fly about 390 miles, roughly an hour in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, and more often in December and June.

- Distance: 390 miles (630 km)
- In the air: about 1 h 5 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 11,207
- Severe reports: 100 (0.9%)
- Gusty days: DEN 155, SLC 105

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Colorado, near Denver, about 5 min after leaving DEN, or 55 min after leaving SLC. There, 1,136 of 2,068 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

## Takeoff and landing

DEN had gusts of 25 knots or more on 155 days in the past year, most often in June (22 days). SLC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 105 days in the past year, most often in July (14 days).

## Questions

### Is DEN to SLC a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the DEN–SLC route filed 11,207 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 100 of 11,207 (0.9%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between DEN and SLC?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, near Denver, about 5 min after leaving DEN, or 55 min after leaving SLC.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly DEN to SLC?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in August, September and October and a bigger share in December and June. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SLC to DEN the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at DEN and SLC?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. DEN had gusts of 25 knots or more on 155 days in the past year, most often in June (22 days). SLC had gusts of 25 knots or more on 105 days in the past year, most often in July (14 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
