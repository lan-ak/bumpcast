# IAH to SEA turbulence

> Houston (IAH) ⇄ Seattle (SEA), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Houston (IAH) and Seattle (SEA) fly about 1,870 miles, roughly 4 hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, and more often in December and May.

- Distance: 1,870 miles (3,010 km)
- In the air: about 3 h 55 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 22,004
- Severe reports: 64 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: IAH 62, SEA 48

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Colorado, near Colorado Springs, about 1 h 40 min after leaving IAH, or 2 h 10 min after leaving SEA. There, 1,024 of 2,722 reports were moderate or stronger. The ground below is high, and wind over mountains makes waves that reach cruising height.

The next most were over Texas, about 1 h 5 min after leaving IAH, or 2 h 45 min after leaving SEA. There, 619 of 1,458 reports were moderate or stronger. At cruising height, bumps usually come from clear-air turbulence near the jet stream, or from clouds and storms.

## Questions

### Is IAH to SEA a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the IAH–SEA route filed 22,004 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 64 of 22,004 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between IAH and SEA?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Colorado, near Colorado Springs, about 1 h 40 min after leaving IAH, or 2 h 10 min after leaving SEA. The next most were over Texas, about 1 h 5 min after leaving IAH, or 2 h 45 min after leaving SEA.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly IAH to SEA?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in July and August and a bigger share in December and May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is SEA to IAH the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from SEA to IAH usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at IAH and SEA?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. IAH had gusts of 25 knots or more on 62 days in the past year, most often in March (12 days). SEA had gusts of 25 knots or more on 48 days in the past year, most often in December (18 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
