# YYZ to YWG turbulence

> Toronto (YYZ) ⇄ Winnipeg (YWG), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Toronto (YYZ) and Winnipeg (YWG) fly about 930 miles, roughly 2 hours in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Michigan, and more often in November, March and June.

- Distance: 930 miles (1,500 km)
- In the air: about 2 h 5 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,154
- Severe reports: 3 (0.3%)
- Gusty days: YYZ 120, YWG 138

## Questions

### Is YYZ to YWG a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the YYZ–YWG route filed 1,154 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 3 of 1,154 (0.3%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. ### When is the smoothest time of year to fly YYZ to YWG?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in January, February and October and a bigger share in November, March and June. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is YWG to YYZ the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight. Flights from YWG to YYZ usually take less time than the way back, with the prevailing westerly winds behind them.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at YYZ and YWG?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. YYZ had gusts of 25 knots or more on 120 days in the past year, most often in January (18 days). YWG had gusts of 25 knots or more on 138 days in the past year, most often in October (15 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
