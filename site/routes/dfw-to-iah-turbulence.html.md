# DFW to IAH turbulence

> Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) ⇄ Houston (IAH), both directions. November 2025 to October 2026.

Flights between Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Houston (IAH) fly about 220 miles, roughly 45 minutes in the air. In the past year, pilots near this route reported moderate bumps most often over Texas, and more often in February, March and May.

- Distance: 220 miles (360 km)
- In the air: about 45 min (typical jet, still air)
- Pilot reports near the route: 1,644
- Severe reports: 15 (0.9%)
- Gusty days: DFW 122, IAH 62

## Where on the route it gets bumpy

Moderate bumps were reported most often over Texas, near Dallas, about 5 min after leaving DFW, or 35 min after leaving IAH. There, 61 of 200 reports were moderate or stronger. Close to the ground, wind over buildings and land stirs the air, and gusty days at DFW add to it.

## Takeoff and landing

DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days). IAH had gusts of 25 knots or more on 62 days in the past year, most often in March (12 days).

## Questions

### Is DFW to IAH a bumpy flight?

Most flights see light chop at most. From November 2025 to October 2026, pilots near the DFW–IAH route filed 1,644 turbulence reports, and severe reports were rare: 15 of 1,644 (0.9%). Pilots report bumpy air more readily than smooth, so the share of flights with strong bumps is lower still. On the day, the weather decides: BumpCast forecasts your exact flight from 48 hours before departure.

### Where is it bumpiest between DFW and IAH?

Pilots reported moderate bumps most often over Texas, near Dallas, about 5 min after leaving DFW, or 35 min after leaving IAH.

### When is the smoothest time of year to fly DFW to IAH?

In the past year, moderate bumps made up a smaller share of reports in August, September and October and a bigger share in February, March and May. Any month can have a smooth or a bumpy day, so the forecast for your flight matters more.

### Is IAH to DFW the same?

Yes. It's the same air, so bumps are reported in the same places, just at different times into the flight.

### Are takeoffs and landings bumpy at DFW and IAH?

On windy days they can be, for a few minutes. DFW had gusts of 25 knots or more on 122 days in the past year, most often in March (21 days). IAH had gusts of 25 knots or more on 62 days in the past year, most often in March (12 days).

## How this page was made

- Period: November 2025 to October 2026. Pilot reports (PIREPs) and airport weather reports (METARs) from the US National Weather Service, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/gis/pireps.php
- Reports from airliners within 60 nautical miles of the direct path, near the height a flight would be there (BumpCast's flight model). Each report counts at the strongest turbulence it names; "moderate to severe" counts as severe.
- Gusty days: days with at least one hourly report of gusts of 25 knots or more.
- Reporting habits differ: smooth air made up 4% of cruise reports at Washington Center but 45% at Miami Center. These numbers show where and when bumps were reported, compared within a route. They aren't the odds for a given flight, and routes aren't ranked against each other.
