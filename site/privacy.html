BumpCast

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

BumpCast forecasts turbulence for flights you add. We collect only what we need to do that, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it to track you across other apps or websites.

No account, no name

BumpCast doesn't ask for your name, email address or phone number. When you open the app it creates an anonymous ID so your flights and forecasts can sync with our servers. It isn't tied to your name or contact details.

What we store

  • Flights you add: flight number, departure and arrival airports, date and time, and the flight's live status (delays, gate and terminal). We use them to find your flight and to compute and update your forecast.
  • Forecasts and notifications we've made for your flights, so the app can show what changed.
  • Push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can send forecast updates. Turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • After-landing ratings ("How bumpy was it?"). We use them, without your identity, to measure and improve forecast accuracy and to publish accuracy stats.
  • Coverage waitlist: the country you asked us to cover, if you tap "Notify me".
  • Onboarding answers, such as how often you fly and how you feel about turbulence, used to tailor the app and the offers we show you.
  • Purchases: whether you have BumpCast Pro or a Flight Pass. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and anonymous sign-in.
  • Fly.io runs the forecast engine.
  • AeroDataBox receives the flight numbers, airports and dates you search for, so we can find your flight and its live status. It doesn't receive your anonymous ID.
  • Superwall runs our onboarding and subscription screens. With your anonymous ID it receives basic device details (model, iOS version, language, region, time zone and an app-specific device identifier), which screens you see and what you buy, your onboarding answers, and a summary of your flights (how many you've saved, and your next flight's route, hours to departure and forecast grade). We use this to choose which screen or offer to show and to measure how well they work.
  • Apple processes purchases and delivers push notifications.

They process data only to provide their service to us. BumpCast doesn't ask for permission to track you, doesn't collect your advertising identifier, and doesn't share your data with data brokers or ad networks.

Location

BumpCast doesn't access your device's location. Forecasts use only the airports you enter.

Keeping and deleting your data

Deleting a flight in the app deletes it and its forecasts from our servers. To delete everything linked to your anonymous ID, email us and we'll delete it within 30 days. Deleting the app removes data stored on your phone.

Children

BumpCast isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.
