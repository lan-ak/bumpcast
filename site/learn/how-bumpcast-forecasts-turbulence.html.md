# How BumpCast forecasts

> Advance, then confirmed.

BumpCast gives an advance turbulence forecast up to two days before your flight, then confirms it about 18 hours before departure and updates it every hour until you fly.

## How does BumpCast forecast turbulence?

Up to two days before your flight, you get an advance forecast. It can still change. About 18 hours before departure it's confirmed with a more detailed forecast along your likely route and altitude, then updated every hour until you fly.

## Does the crew have its own forecast?

Your crew has their own forecasts and live reports, and adjusts on the day.

## How exact is the forecast?

We assume a direct route and a typical climb, cruise and descent, so treat it as a guide, not a guarantee.

## Good to know

We'll notify you when your forecast is confirmed and if anything changes.
