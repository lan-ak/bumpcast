# Turbulence Explained: Why Flights Get Bumpy

> Why flights get bumpy, what the crew is doing, and what you'll feel. Short, factual explainers from the BumpCast app: clear-air turbulence, mountain waves, clouds, windy takeoffs and landings, and why planes are built for it.

## Why it gets bumpy

- [Clear-air turbulence](https://bumpcast.app/learn/clear-air-turbulence.html.md): Clear-air turbulence is bumpy air at cruising height, often in clear skies, where the fast jet stream flows next to slower air. It's usually light chop.
- [Mountain waves](https://bumpcast.app/learn/mountain-wave-turbulence.html.md): Mountain waves form when strong wind blows across mountains and makes waves in the air, like water over rocks in a stream. They can reach cruising height.
- [Around clouds](https://bumpcast.app/learn/turbulence-near-clouds.html.md): Warm air rising inside and around big clouds stirs the air nearby. Bumps in puffy clouds are usually brief, and pilots fly around storms, often by many miles.
- [Near the ground](https://bumpcast.app/learn/turbulence-on-takeoff-and-landing.html.md): Close to the ground, wind flows over buildings, hills and warm land, so the lowest few thousand feet are often a bit bumpy. It usually lasts only a few minutes.
- [Gusty at the airport](https://bumpcast.app/learn/gusty-takeoffs-and-landings.html.md): On a windy day, the wind near the ground speeds up and slows down over buildings and land. You feel it for a few minutes as you climb out and on the way down.

## Good to know

- [Planes are built for it](https://bumpcast.app/learn/planes-are-built-for-turbulence.html.md): Airliners are designed and tested to handle forces well beyond anything turbulence produces in normal flight. Turbulence is a comfort issue, not a structural one.
- [Why the seatbelt matters](https://bumpcast.app/learn/why-the-seatbelt-matters.html.md): Almost all turbulence injuries happen to people who aren't buckled in. Keep your seatbelt loosely fastened whenever you're seated, even with the sign off.
- [Light, moderate, strong](https://bumpcast.app/learn/turbulence-levels.html.md): Light chop is small rhythmic bumps, moderate is noticeable jolts, and strong is hard jolts that push you against your belt. Most flights see light chop at most.
- [How BumpCast forecasts](https://bumpcast.app/learn/how-bumpcast-forecasts-turbulence.html.md): BumpCast gives an advance turbulence forecast up to two days before your flight, then confirms it about 18 hours before departure and updates it every hour until you fly.
