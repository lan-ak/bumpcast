# Around clouds

> Rising air near big clouds.

Warm air rising inside and around big clouds stirs the air nearby. Bumps in puffy clouds are usually brief, and pilots fly around storms, often by many miles.

## Why is it bumpy around clouds?

Warm air rising inside and around big clouds stirs the air nearby.

## How do pilots avoid storms?

Pilots see storms on radar and fly around them, often by many miles. That's why your route might curve.

## What does turbulence near clouds feel like?

Short bumps as you pass through puffy clouds; usually brief.

## Good to know

Avoiding storms is routine; air traffic control expects planes to route around them.
