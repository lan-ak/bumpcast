# Mountain waves

> Wind rolling over high ground.

Mountain waves form when strong wind blows across mountains and makes waves in the air, like water over rocks in a stream. They can reach cruising height.

## What causes mountain wave turbulence?

Strong wind blowing across mountains makes waves in the air, much like water flowing over rocks in a stream. They can reach cruising height.

## How do pilots handle mountain waves?

Crews know where mountain waves are likely and plan altitudes around them. They slow to a calmer speed if needed.

## What do mountain waves feel like?

A slow rise and fall, sometimes with light chop.

## Good to know

The bumps follow the terrain, so they usually pass once you're beyond the mountains.
