# Near the ground

> Climbing and descending.

Close to the ground, wind flows over buildings, hills and warm land, so the lowest few thousand feet are often a bit bumpy. It usually lasts only a few minutes.

## Why is it bumpy after takeoff and before landing?

Close to the ground, wind flows over buildings, hills and warm patches of land, so the lowest few thousand feet are often a bit bumpy.

## What do pilots do near the ground?

Takeoff and landing are planned with wind in mind. Pilots keep the seatbelt sign on until the air settles.

## What do bumps near the ground feel like?

Small bumps during the climb or the final descent, which ease as you go higher or touch down.

## Good to know

This is normal on many flights and usually lasts only a few minutes.
