# Why the seatbelt matters

> The one thing that keeps you safe.

Almost all turbulence injuries happen to people who aren't buckled in. Keep your seatbelt loosely fastened whenever you're seated, even with the sign off.

## Who gets hurt in turbulence?

Almost all turbulence injuries happen to people who aren't buckled in, usually while standing or walking.

## Why do flight attendants sit down?

When flight attendants sit down, it's routine caution, not an emergency.

## What does turbulence feel like with your belt on?

With your belt on, even strong bumps are just uncomfortable.

## Good to know

Keep it loosely fastened whenever you're seated, even with the sign off.
