# Light, moderate, strong

> What each one feels like.

Light chop is small rhythmic bumps, moderate is noticeable jolts, and strong is hard jolts that push you against your belt. Most flights see light chop at most.

## What are the levels of turbulence?

Light chop is small rhythmic bumps. Moderate is noticeable jolts. Strong is hard jolts that push you against your belt.

## How do pilots handle moderate and strong turbulence?

Pilots work to avoid moderate and strong bumps, changing altitude or route when they can.

## What does each level feel like?

Light: your drink ripples. Moderate: walking is tricky and drinks can spill. Strong: rare, and your belt keeps you safe.

## Good to know
