Find your flight

Type the flight number. That's it.

Enter B6 23 and the day you fly, and BumpCast fills in the airports and times. Or pick your airports and choose from that day's nonstops. Add connecting flights in a row.

  • Live status: delays, gates, terminals, cancellations and diversions
  • One gentle alert when your flight is delayed
  • Free for every flight you add

Bumps along the way

What other trackers don't tell you.

Turbulence is weather, and weather can be forecast. BumpCast maps your route from climb to cruise to descent and shows when each bumpy stretch starts, how long it lasts, and what it will feel like.

  • Smooth Your drink stays still.
  • Light chop Your drink ripples.
  • Moderate Drinks can spill. Stay seated.
  • Strong Rare. Your belt keeps you safe.

In the air

A countdown to every bump, on your Lock Screen.

Once you've taken off, the Live Activity and Dynamic Island count down to the next bumpy stretch, with a heads-up two minutes before it starts. Your forecast is saved before you board, so it all works in airplane mode.

Honest about certainty

Sharper as you get closer.

An advance forecast 48 hours before departure, updates at 24, 6 and 2 hours out, and a confirmed forecast on the day you fly. Each update says what changed and why. Delayed? Your forecast follows the new departure time, with one alert for both.

Calm, on cue

Built for when the seatbelt sign comes on.

Guided breathing paced to your next bumpy stretch. Short, factual explainers on what causes bumps at cruise, over mountains and near clouds, and why planes are built for it. No red alerts, no scare graphics.

Turbulence map

See the whole sky, at every height.

Turbulence across the country by altitude, now and up to six hours ahead, with your route drawn on top. Pro adds live pilot reports of what planes are actually feeling.

After you land

We check our work.

Tell us how bumpy it really felt. Every rating is checked against the forecast, and we publish our accuracy in the app.
