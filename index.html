A flight tracker first

Everything you'd want from a tracker. Free.

Add your flight by number and BumpCast keeps an eye on it for you, from the gate to the ground.

Find your flight by number

Type AC 1504 or pick from the day's nonstops on your route. Airports and times fill in for you. Connections too.

Live status and delay alerts

Delays, gates, terminals, cancellations and diversions, with one gentle alert when your flight is delayed.

A forecast that moves with you

Delayed? Your turbulence forecast follows the new departure time, and you get one alert for both.

On your Lock Screen

A Live Activity and Dynamic Island countdown to your next bumpy stretch, with a heads-up two minutes before it starts.

What other trackers don't tell you

How the flight will actually feel.

Turbulence is weather, and weather can be forecast. BumpCast maps your route from climb to cruise to descent and describes every stretch in one of four levels, shown as a glass of water. No alarm colours, no jargon.

Smooth

Your drink stays still.

Light chop

Your drink ripples.

Moderate

Drinks can spill. Stay seated.

Strong

Rare. Your belt keeps you safe.

Calm, on cue

Built for the moment the seatbelt sign comes on.

Breathing, paced to the bumps

A guided breathing exercise timed to your next bumpy stretch, under a sky that matches your departure time.

Honest about certainty

An advance forecast 48 hours out, updates at 24, 6 and 2 hours, and a confirmed forecast on the day. Each one says how sure it is and what changed.

Explained, not just predicted

Short, factual explainers: what causes bumps at cruise, over mountains and near clouds, and why planes are built for it. Saved for airplane mode.
